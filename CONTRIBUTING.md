# Contributing

Thank you for helping make AI Audiobook Reader more accessible.

The project is currently in an early planning stage. Before making a large change, open an issue to discuss the idea.

Good early contributions include:
- accessibility ideas
- UI/UX suggestions
- PDF/text extraction improvements
- Android development
- audio engineering
- documentation
- testing

Please keep contributions focused on the project's goal: helping people who struggle with reading access stories through high-quality audio.
