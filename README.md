# AI Audiobook Reader

An open-source Android app that turns books and other readable content into expressive, personality-driven audiobooks.

## Vision

Make reading more accessible for people who struggle with traditional books by letting them listen to stories as natural, expressive performances rather than robotic text-to-speech.

## Planned features

- PDF book import
- URL/web content import
- Natural AI narration with personality
- Emotion and scene-aware delivery
- Character-aware voices
- Audiobook player with progress and bookmarks
- Downloadable offline audiobooks
- Efficient audio storage (planned: Opus)
- Chapter-based audio generation and downloads
- Free/open-source core application

## Project principles

1. Accessibility first.
2. Keep the core app free and open source.
3. Prefer privacy-friendly designs.
4. Make the narration feel human and story-focused.
5. Build in small, understandable steps.

## Copyright

Users are responsible for ensuring that books and other content they import or generate audio from are content they are legally allowed to use. The project should not be designed to bypass DRM or distribute copyrighted books.
